PP

Polypropylene

Products that are suitable for special purposes

Polypropylene(PP), one of the most commonly used plastics, is a synthetic resin made by polymerizing propylene. Its types vary depending on its properties and processing methods. Its excellent tensile strength and impact strength make it widely used in multiple areas, including films, textiles, automotive parts, and more. LG Chem produces and supplies PP products that are suitable for special purposes such as injection molding and fiber.
